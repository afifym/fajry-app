Privacy Policy

Fajry

On your device

Fajry uses your location on your device to calculate Fajr Time. Location, alarm settings, and prayer streaks are stored on your device and are not uploaded.

Crash reports

In production, the app may send anonymous crash and performance reports to Sentry so bugs can be fixed. These reports are not linked to your name, email, or account.

What we do not do

  • We do not create accounts.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or tracking.
